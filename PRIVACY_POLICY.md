# Privacy Policy for Arctic Sidebar

**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026  
**Extension:** Arctic Sidebar (Google Chrome Extension)  
**Developer:** Sumit Sabhnani  

Arctic Sidebar is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data and how permissions are utilized.

---

### 1. Summary: Zero Data Collection

* **Arctic Sidebar does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data.**
* **Zero external servers:** The extension does not connect to any external server, cloud database, analytics service, or advertising network.
* **100% on-device processing:** All tabs, bookmarks, history searches, and settings are handled exclusively within your local Google Chrome browser.

---

### 2. Permissions & How They Are Used

Arctic Sidebar only requests the minimum necessary permissions required to provide vertical tab and workspace functionality:

* **`sidePanel`**: Allows the extension interface to be rendered inside Google Chrome's native Side Panel.
* **`bookmarks`**: Allows you to view, organize, create, rename, and manage your pinned tabs and workspace folders directly from the sidebar. Your bookmarks remain standard Google Chrome bookmarks and sync only via your own Google Account if Chrome Sync is enabled.
* **`tabs`**: Required to display open tabs in the sidebar, activate/switch between tabs, close tabs, hibernate/sleep background tabs to save system memory, and bind open tabs to bookmarks.
* **`storage`**: Uses Chrome's local storage (`chrome.storage.local` and `chrome.storage.session`) strictly to remember your preferences (such as your chosen color theme, dark/light mode preference, auto-archive timer, and collapsed folder states).
* **`history`**: Used solely on the New Tab Command Bar page to provide instant local autocomplete suggestions from your browsing history while you type. History data is queried locally on your machine and is never transmitted anywhere.
* **`favicon`**: Uses Google Chrome's internal favicon provider (`chrome://favicon/`) to display website icons next to your tabs, bookmarks, and search suggestions.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Arctic Sidebar does not include any third-party tracking scripts, cookies, telemetry, or analytics (such as Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Sentry). No network requests are made by the extension in the background.

---

### 4. Chrome Web Store Compliance

Arctic Sidebar adheres strictly to the **Google Chrome Web Store User Data Policy**, including the Limited Use requirements.
